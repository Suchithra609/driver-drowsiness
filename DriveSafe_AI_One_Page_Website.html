How DriveSafe AI works

01 · Face and eye analysis

A browser-based face model estimates eye-blink signals from camera frames.

02 · Sustained closure check

The system checks whether both eyes remain closed longer than the selected delay.

03 · Alarm and event log

A warning sound can play and a timestamped event is added to the session history.
